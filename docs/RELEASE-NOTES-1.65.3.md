# Portfolio Architect v1.65.3 — visible plan and policy evidence

The investment-plan dashboard explains source freshness and health blockers beside the plan rather than requiring entity inspection. It separately explains a current `no_eligible_purchase`, unavailable investment reserve, or execution-cost deferral. A stale source takes precedence over an older execution result so the card does not imply an old recommendation is current.

Portfolio policy findings now display their rule, observed value, and expected value with the fund name. The `savings_plan_required` error explains that dated provider route evidence must be verified and updated. Existing individual finding entities remain available below the explanation for detailed review. Both English and German dashboards are generated from the shared source and localized catalogs.

This is presentation-only. DKB and Trade Republic cash and portfolio statements retain their source and freshness rules. DKB FinTS holdings and cash shadows remain research-only, do not feed the planner, and are not automatically compared with CSV. No authorization, acquisition, refresh, or planning logic changes.
