# Portfolio Architect v1.66.0

This release introduces an **explicit DKB acquisition source switch**. CSV stays authoritative after upgrade. In DKB admin Ingress, a complete manual FinTS read made with v1.66.0 can prepare the one authorized depot and the selected EUR cash account. Selecting FinTS for both capabilities requires a separate confirmation. Neither a manual read nor account discovery switches authority by itself.

Each FinTS holding must have a valid ISIN, quantity, bounded EUR total value, and an independent observation time. The booked EUR cash amount has its own observation time, bank balance date and keyed binding to the selected account. Only normalized bounded evidence is retained privately; credentials, TANs, account inventory and raw bank responses remain transient. Position names are displayed as ISIN labels and their type as `other` because FinTS research does not supply a trustworthy source-neutral classification. Pending transactions can make booked cash differ from spendable cash.

**Both observations expire 14 days after their actual read times.** The limit is fixed for DKB FinTS and cannot be extended by PA's generic live API freshness setting. A missing, corrupt, future, non-EUR, account-mismatched or expired observation makes the FinTS Gateway snapshot unavailable. A failed subsequent manual read can leave an earlier valid FinTS observation available until its own expiry. CSV is never selected automatically. The operator may explicitly switch back to complete CSV evidence.

CSV holdings, CSV cash, the private FinTS evidence and the FinTS canonical cache have separate files. Switching or restarting cannot make a FinTS cache appear to be CSV data. The new private FinTS evidence and cache are excluded from Home Assistant App backups; a restore with FinTS selected remains unavailable until another complete read or an explicit CSV switch. The existing 24-hour research-shadow status and five-minute detailed review remain separate from the 14-day authority rule.

The source switch changes the canonical provider snapshot and the PA plan once selected. The existing investment cash authorization policy applies to the chosen cash source. No background bank polling, automatic approval, money movement, or trading operation is added. v1.65.9 cannot understand a v1.66.0 FinTS selection; explicitly switch back to CSV before a downgrade.
